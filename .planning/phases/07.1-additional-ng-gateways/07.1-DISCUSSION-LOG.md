### Adapter fit follow-up — confirmation backstop

| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Verify-on-return + poll backstop | verifyTransaction primary; consume webhook when it exists; polling backstop; no new infra | ✓ |

webhook support on Interswitch/Remita. Idempotency guard still makes first signal win. → D-15.
